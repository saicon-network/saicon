Operating system
  The image is based on Alpine Linux (alpine:3.20) and adds the Alpine packages
  ca-certificates, bash and jq. alpine-packages.txt lists every installed package
  with its version and license, as recorded by apk when the image was built.
  These packages are unmodified Alpine builds. The complete corresponding source
  of every copyleft-licensed one among them (GPL, LGPL, MPL: busybox, bash,
  readline, apk-tools and others) — its build recipe, patches and upstream
  archives, for exactly the versions in this image — is attached to the image's
  release as saicon-image-alpine-sources_<version>.tar.gz:
  https://github.com/saicon-network/saicon/releases
